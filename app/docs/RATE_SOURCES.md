# Bundled rate sources

Rate-based results are estimates and record the configuration version used. Formula code does not contain rate tables.

## Salary tax

- Tax Year 2025–26 salaried-person slabs are based on Pakistan's Finance Act 2025 and FBR's Tax Year 2026 rate material.
- Official reference: https://www.fbr.gov.pk/Categ/Finance-Acts/620
- Official rate cards: https://ipv6.fbr.gov.pk/withholding-taxes-rate-card/174298/174301

The current basic calculator applies the standard salaried slabs. Individual credits, exemptions, special treatment, surcharge, employer adjustments, and non-salary classifications require professional review and belong to the advanced calculator.

## Electricity

- The bundled non-protected residential estimate uses the GOP-applicable variable charges in NEPRA's February 2026 national uniform schedule.
- Official schedule: https://nepra.org.pk/tariff/Tariff/Ex-WAPDA%20DISCOS/2026/TRF-100%20XWDISCOS%20AND%20KE%20RATIONALIZATION%20OF%20TARIFF%2011-02-2026%202935-57.pdf

The simple estimate cannot reproduce every bill. Protected/lifeline eligibility, sanctioned load, time-of-use meters, FCA, QTA, duties, provincial charges, TV fee, arrears, minimum charges, provider rules, and later notifications may change the total. The screen therefore accepts an adjustable tax/adjustment percentage and labels its output as an estimate.

## Solar, fuel, and metals

These values are volatile and location-dependent. Solar currently uses clearly labeled editable planning assumptions. Fuel and Nisab inputs are entered manually rather than silently populated with a stale price. A future signed configuration adapter can update bundled values without changing formulas.
