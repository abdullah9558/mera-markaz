# MeraMarkaz V1 release matrix

## Included

- Expense tracking and monthly/category budgets
- Udhaar ledgers and payments
- Savings goals and contributions
- Salary tax, electricity, fuel, and Zakat calculators
- Expense/Udhaar/savings history and global search
- Email/password, Google and Facebook sign-in, guest sessions, encrypted cloud sync
- Local encrypted backup/export and restore
- Profile, English/Urdu, light/dark/system appearance
- Privacy policy, terms, feedback, support, and data controls

## Excluded from the V1 package and navigation

- Income tracking
- Notifications and Android widgets
- Markaz AI and Pakistan Live
- Watchlists, receipts/OCR, voice entry
- Family/household, committee, net worth, financial health, safe-to-spend
- Freelancer, financing, inflation intelligence, solar, property, and recurring bills

The complete pre-V1 implementation is preserved in Git tag
`full-feature-pre-v1-2026-09-01`. Future modules can be restored deliberately
from that tag after their product, privacy, and store-readiness reviews.
